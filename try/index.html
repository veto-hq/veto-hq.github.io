Try Veto on your Seeker

For Seeker owners testing Veto before October 8, 2026.

Your AI agent can only ask; the program pays or refuses, and every refusal is saved on the blockchain. A Hold vault makes big withdrawals wait, and a second key can stop them.

This is Solana devnet test money with no value.

5-minute setup

  1. Download the APK and install Veto on your Seeker.
  2. Set your Seed Vault wallet to devnet.
  3. Get devnet SOL and devnet USDC.

Try three things

  1. Give a demo agent a rule. Write a rule for a demo agent, then watch a payment and a refusal in Decisions.
  2. Try a Hold vault. Set one up with a small amount of test money. See a withdrawal to a new address held, then use the second key to stop it.
  3. Check your agent's grade. Look at your agent's grade in the Agents tab.
