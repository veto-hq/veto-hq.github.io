Try Veto: your first refusal in about 5 minutes

Veto lets you set spending rules for an AI agent with your Solana wallet: who it may pay, how much per payment and how much in total.

Every request the agent makes is paid or refused on chain against your rule, and you can read the record on your phone.

This build runs on Solana devnet only. Everything here is test money with no value.

Never share your recovery phrase or private key with anyone, including us. Veto will never ask for it.

What you need

  • A Seeker, or another Android phone with a Solana wallet that supports Mobile Wallet Adapter (Seed Vault on Seeker).
  • That wallet set to devnet.
  • About 5 minutes. Faucets and confirmations are sometimes slow.

You do not need your own agent. Veto can make a test agent on your phone.

Five steps

  1. Install the app.

    Download veto-devnet-1.0.0-build28.apk from the latest release. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks. The release page lists the file's SHA-256 if you want to check the download.

  2. Get free devnet SOL and devnet USDC.

    Copy your wallet address.

    • SOL for fees: open faucet.solana.com, choose devnet and paste your address.
    • USDC: open faucet.circle.com, choose Solana Devnet and paste the same address.

    Wait until both show up in your wallet.

  3. Open Veto and approve a small rule.

    Read the short intro or tap Skip to connect wallet, then connect and approve in your wallet. Tap Add your agent, then Create a test agent on this phone. Give it a name and tap Review the rule.

    Already set up from an earlier build? Open Rules, tap Write a rule, keep Payment rule and leave Agent empty. Approving then creates a test agent on this phone. Fill in the rule as below. After you approve, Send two test requests is on the rule's page.

    On the rule screen:

    • Payee: paste your own wallet address (the same one you used at the faucets). The test payment then comes back to you.
    • Most per payment: 1
    • Most in total, ever: 5 (keep it at or below the USDC you hold)
    • Leave Purpose and Rule ends in as they are, or change them.

    Wait for the checks to clear, then Press and hold to approve rule and approve in your wallet. The 5 USDC moves into the rule's own account, which you control.

  4. Tap Send two test requests.

    The button appears on the screen that says your rule is live, right after you approve. (Later you can find it on the rule's detail page.)

    Veto shows you exactly what it will send: one payment of half your per-payment limit (0.5 USDC), and one request just above the limit (1.000001 USDC). Tap Send requests.

    If the test agent is low on fee money, your wallet asks you to send it 0.01 devnet SOL once. That is the only thing you sign here. The phone signs both requests with the test agent's key.

  5. See one paid, one refused.

    Two lines appear:

    • Paid 0.5 USDC to the payee.
    • Refused 1.000001 USDC: over per-payment maximum.

    Each has a link to the transaction on the devnet explorer. Open Decisions to see both in your record.

That refusal is the point: the agent asked for more than you allowed, and the chain said no. No money moved for it.

Keep it running: get charges from our demo agent

Our demo agent asks every rule that names it to pay for a small electric car top-up (6 kWh) every six hours. The amount follows the Swedish electricity spot price, so it changes with the hour. On 29 September 2026 it ranged from about 0.75 to 1.21 USDC.

    Open Rules, tap Write a rule and keep Payment rule. Paste this in Agent:

    6YwqYUj4Kyy8dnPss34jMWgKAtLGAghmA1dRgYUGSV5w
  2. Set the payee and the limits.

    Paste this in Payee. The demo agent skips rules with any other payee.

    6i99pFwsoV9wBWSaNtXxpXgCWjpCkMbZ4UE6T4cSPdCG
    • Most per payment: 1
    • Most in total, ever: 10 (these 10 USDC move into the rule's own account, so get more from the faucet first if you need to)
  3. Approve it.

    Press and hold to approve rule and approve in your wallet.

What to expect:

  • A request around 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 Stockholm time. It can arrive later than that, and no request is sent when there is no price.
  • Cheaper hours are paid. Pricier hours ask for more than 1 USDC and are refused, and nothing moves. On most days you see both.
  • If a request is refused for going over your per-payment limit, open it in Decisions, use Press and hold to allow this one payment and approve in your wallet. Do it before the next request is paid; after that the app no longer offers it. On its next run (00:00, 06:00, 12:00 or 18:00 Stockholm time), the demo agent sends the same request again and it is paid once, as long as there is a price at that run and the payment still fits what is left in the rule. Your limit stays the same.
  • A notification on your phone when a request is paid or refused, if you allow notifications (Turn notifications on on the Overview screen). The phone checks about every 15 minutes, and battery saving can delay it.
  • To end the requests, open the rule and use Stop the rule.

This is devnet test money with no value, like everything else on this page.

If something goes wrong

  • The button is missing. It only shows for an active devnet payment rule that uses this phone's test agent. Rules for other agents need that agent running separately.
  • "Wrong network" or the wallet will not sign. Check that your wallet is on devnet.
  • A request failed. Check the explorer link before trying again. A sent request can still land.
